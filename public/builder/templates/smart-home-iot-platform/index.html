How AuraHome built a synchronized
home environment? 💎

Glowing Ambience

Vocal assistants like Alexa or Google Home can serve as the primary link for all smart hardware. They can chat with and adjust smart bulbs, sensors, gate locks, feeds, and sound gear using verbal orders, building a fluid and hands-off smart living world.

Glowing Ambience

Vivid lighting networks, such as smart LEDs, plugs, or strips, can be integrated with other tools in the network. For instance, they can be set to activate or dim based on sensor triggers, timed scenes, or even linked with other smart units like media for immersive visual effects.

Media Integration

Smart TVs, players, audio and casting tools can be combined into the smart home environment for fluid digital experiences. They can link with lighting setups to build moods, be managed via vocal prompts, or be paired with other hardware for a truly cinematic residential layout.
