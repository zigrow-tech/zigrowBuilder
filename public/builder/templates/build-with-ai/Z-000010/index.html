Frequently
asked questions

What are the requirements to enroll?

To enroll in our programs, you need to be at least 17 years old and have completed your secondary education.

How long does the training take?

The duration of training varies by program, but typically ranges from six months to two years, depending on the license.

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes, we provide various financing options and scholarships to help students manage their tuition costs.

What is the pass rate for students?

Our students have a 99% pass rate for both theoretical and practical examinations, reflecting our commitment to quality training.

Can I train part-time?

Yes, we offer flexible training schedules to accommodate students who wish to pursue their education part-time.

Are there any age limits for training?

There are no upper age limits for training; however, students must meet the minimum age requirement for each program.
